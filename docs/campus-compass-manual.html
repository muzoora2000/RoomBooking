Campus Compass

A smart room booking and campus navigation system that helps every student, lecturer, and administrator at Cavendish University Uganda find the right space, make a booking in seconds, and arrive verified — all from any phone or laptop.

📱 Progressive Web App 🏫 Lecture Room Booking 📚 Revision Room Booking ✅ QR Check-In 🔔 Live Notifications 🖥 Door Display Screen 👤 Multi-Role Access
🎓 Built for Cavendish University Uganda 🌍 IEEE CUU Student Branch Innovation 📅 2026
01 — The Problem

What We Are Solving

Every day at Cavendish University Uganda, students and lecturers waste valuable time because there is no reliable way to find and book a room.

🏫 Lecture Room Clashes

Lecturers arrive to find their assigned room already occupied by another class. Timetable clashes are only discovered when two groups show up at the same door, causing disruption and wasted contact hours.

📚 The Revision Room Struggle

Students organising group study and revision sessions — especially during exam season — have no way to check which rooms are vacant or reserve a space in advance. They wander the campus losing study time they cannot afford to lose.

📋 Manual, Slow Coordination

Class representatives must physically visit the admin office or make phone calls just to request a room. Notice boards and spreadsheets are updated too slowly to reflect real availability.

🚪 Empty Rooms, No Visibility

Rooms sit empty because nobody knows they are available. The university has the physical space — the problem is that this information is invisible to the people who need it most.

02 — The Solution

How Campus Compass Works

Campus Compass is a Progressive Web App (PWA) — no download, no installation. Any student or staff member opens it in their browser and immediately has access to live room availability, bookings, schedules, and notifications.

🔍

Find Any Space — Live

Search lecture halls, seminar rooms, labs, and discussion rooms by building, capacity, or equipment. Availability is shown in real time — no guessing, no walking around.

📚

Instant Revision Room Booking

Students can browse all available spaces, filter by group size and facilities, and reserve a revision or study room in seconds — directly from their phone, without visiting any office. The system automatically prevents double bookings and notifies the student with a confirmation.

🏫

Clash-Free Lecture Scheduling

Priority rules, capacity checks, and database-level exclusion constraints guarantee that two sessions can never occupy the same room at the same time. Timetable conflicts are caught automatically before they cause disruption.

📱

QR Check-In at the Door

Every room has a door display screen. Students and lecturers scan a rotating QR code on arrival to confirm their attendance — secure, instant, and privacy-friendly.

🎓

Physical, Online & Hybrid Sessions

The system supports all three modes. Online meeting links and passcodes are delivered securely only to authorised participants — they never appear on public displays or notice boards.

🔔

Smart Notifications

Booking confirmations, reminders, cancellations, and no-show alerts are delivered via email, SMS (Africa's Talking), and browser push notifications — so no one is ever caught off guard.

🖥

Room Door Display Screen

Each room's display shows the current session, the next scheduled booking, live occupancy status, and a QR code for check-in. Administrators can deploy any screen (TV, tablet, monitor) as a door display with a single setup step.

⚙️

Admin Control Dashboard

Department administrators manage rooms, timetables, users, bookings, and notifications from a single dashboard. System administrators have full oversight across all departments and facilities.

03 — How To Use It

Step-by-Step User Journey

👩‍🎓 Student Booking a Revision Room

1

Open Campus Compass

Visit the app URL on any phone or laptop browser — no installation needed.

2

Log in with Student ID

Enter your university ID number and password. A one-time PIN is sent for verification.

3

Browse Available Rooms

Tap "Find a room", filter by date, time, capacity, and type (seminar room, lab, etc.).

4

Book in Seconds

Select a room, enter the session name and group size, and confirm. The system checks for clashes instantly.

5

Receive Confirmation

A confirmation notification is sent via email and SMS with all booking details.

6

Check In on Arrival

Scan the QR code on the room's door display screen to confirm attendance.

👨‍🏫 Lecturer Confirming a Lecture Room

1

Log in

Sign in with your staff ID. Your upcoming lectures appear on the home screen immediately.

2

View Today's Schedule

See all your assigned rooms, times, and lecture details — before leaving home.

3

Get Reminders

Automatic reminders are sent 30 minutes before each session via SMS and push notification.

4

Arrive and Check In

Scan the door QR code. The room display updates to show your lecture as active.

5

Share Online Link (if hybrid)

For hybrid/online sessions, a secure meeting link is sent only to enrolled students.

07 — Technology

Built for Africa, Designed to Scale

Campus Compass is built with proven, production-grade technology and deployed on free-tier cloud services — meaning it costs very little to run and can scale to the entire university without significant IT investment.

Node.js 20 Express API PostgreSQL Neon (Cloud DB) Vercel (Frontend) Render (Backend) Progressive Web App Africa's Talking SMS Web Push Notifications QR Code Check-In GitHub CI/CD Africa/Kampala Timezone

Why a PWA?

A Progressive Web App works on any phone or laptop browser — Android, iPhone, Windows, or Chromebook. No Play Store, no App Store, no storage space needed. Students on limited data plans benefit from intelligent caching that makes the app work even with poor connectivity. This removes every barrier to adoption.
